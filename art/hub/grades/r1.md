### Concrete Fix Lists (Top 3 Priority Fixes per Asset)

#### 1. Sell Stall (`pdv4_sell_s61.png` vs `r1_sell.png`)
1. **Base Plinth & Drawers**: Raise stall on a dark cedar deck (`12x10x1.5` studs); add 4 front-facing drawer faces (`2.5x1x0.2` studs, plum-tinted dark oak `#4E3733`) with brass stud pull knobs (`0.3x0.3` studs, `#E5C158`).
2. **Awning Structure & Valance**: Thicken the 4 corner columns to `1.5x1.5` studs with notched interlocking cap joints; rebuild the scalloped valance with 3D relief drops (`1` stud drop, soft rose-pink `#E898A8` and ivory cream `#FFF5E4`) rather than flat sawtooth blocks.
3. **Cantilevered Sign & Center Lantern**: Remove the loose floor post; mount the "SELL" board (`4x1.5x0.3` studs, butter-yellow font on dark cedar) cantilevered directly from the right-front column with an interlocking crossbar (`0.5x0.5x3` studs); hang a central glowing brass lantern (`Color3.fromRGB(255, 215, 110)`, `PointLight Range 10`) under the awning ridge.

#### 2. Forge (`pdv4_forge_s61.png` vs `r1_forge.png`)
1. **Gable Roof Pitch**: Rebuild the roof from a front-facing ramp into a true side-to-side perpendicular A-frame gable (`45°` pitch, teal shingles `#3B7A75`) with thick dark-walnut bargeboards (`1x1` stud trim) overhanging the wall by `1.5` studs.
2. **Chimney Relocation & Tint**: Move the chimney to the side/rear wall or give it a stepped mantle (`6x4` stud base tapering to `3x3` stud flue); desaturate brick tint from harsh salmon to cool lilac-grey stone (`#9A93A8`); recess the fireplace hearth `2` studs deep with a warm orange fire glow (`PointLight Range 14`, `Brightness 2.5`).
3. **Workshop Depth & Quench Trough**: Carve out a recessed open workshop alcove (`5` studs deep) under the second-story overhang; add a proper water trough (`3x1.5x1.5` studs, stone-brick frame with translucent cyan water) and heavy tree-stump anvil (`radius 1.5`, height `2` studs).

#### 3. Trophy Hall (`pdv4_trophy_s61.png` vs `r1_trophy.png`)
1. **Facade Scale & Arched Portal**: Widen facade from `14` to `24` studs; replace the flat plank square door with an arched double door (`6x9` studs) framed by extruded stone voussoir arch blocks (`1` stud relief, cool grey `#8B8699`).
2. **Three Curved Skulls**: Remove the cubic cow block; model three carved monster skulls across the roofline (center skull `5x4` studs with curled ram horns sweeping back; two flanking horned skulls `3.5x3` studs angled at `30°` along the roof eaves).
3. **Pennant Bunting & Brazier**: Replace the flat diamond flags with 3 swallowtail pennants in pastel rose (`#E0849D`) with cream heart emblems, suspended along a horizontal timber rail (`0.6x0.6x20` studs); add a stone pedestal fire brazier (`2x2x3` studs) flanking the doorway.

#### 4. Guest Cabins (`pdv4_cabin_s62.png` vs `r1_cabin.png`)
1. **Corner Log Saddles**: Break the planar cardboard look by adding interlocking 3D log ends (`protruding 1x1x1.5` stud rounded blocks) alternating at every corner joint.
2. **Gabled Porch Portico**: Replace the flat horizontal plank slab over the door with a pitched A-frame gabled canopy (`5x4` studs, `40°` slope, moss shingles `#508A5E`) supported by two diagonal timber knee-braces (`0.6x0.6x2.5` studs).
3. **Side Stone Chimney & Asymmetry**: Anchor an exterior fieldstone chimney stack (`4x3` studs base tapering to `2.5x2.5` studs, height `16` studs, cobblestone `#7C7585`) on the right wall; differentiate the two cabins with unique flower box colors and porch props so they don't look cloned.

#### 5. Incubator Greenhouse (`pdv4_incubator_s61.png` vs `r1_incubator.png`)
1. **Glass Transparency & Clarity**: Increase glass `Transparency` to `0.50` and strip the dense crosshatch pixel texture so the interior is clearly visible; retain subtle edge mullions and faint diagonal specular streaks instead.
2. **Twig Nests & Speckled Eggs**: Replace the flat orange/brown discs with 3-tiered circular twig nests (`Part` rings with bark texture, `#B58A57`); scale eggs to `3x2` studs in smooth pastel gradients (`#F7CAD0` rose, `#C8E6C9` mint, `#FFF3B0` butter) with tiny speckle details.
3. **Perimeter Succulents & Drop Lanterns**: Place 4 terracotta pots (`1x1` stud, `#C66B4B`) with pastel green voxel succulents along the perimeter low stone sill; suspend 2 pendant lanterns on thin iron drop-rods (`0.15` studs) from the roof ridge beam.

#### 6. Notice Board + Relic Chest (`pdv4_notice_s61.png` vs `r1_notice.png`)
1. **Layered Parchment Bounties**: Delete the rigid grid of identical cards; place 6–8 overlapping parchment notices of varying sizes (`1.5x2`, `1.2x1.5` studs, tilted `3°–8°`, aged cream `#FFF8E7`) with illustrated sketch decals and red wax seal dots (`#A32828`).
2. **Gilded Relic Chest Overhaul**: Redesign the chest (`3.5x2.5x2.2` studs) with reinforced gold bandings (`#E8B838`, `Metal`), an arched dome lid, and an interior point light (`Color3.fromRGB(255, 230, 130)`, `Range 8`, `Brightness 2`) with glowing dust particles leaking from the seam.
3. **Roof Canopy & Cleanup**: Remove the extraneous rainbow signboard; extend the notice board shingle roof forward by `1.5` studs overhang, adding an angled iron bracket lantern on the right post.

#### 7. Campfire (`pdv4_campfire_s62.png` vs `r1_campfire.png`)
1. **A-Frame Cooking Spit**: Construct a rustic timber tripod (`three 0.8x0.8x9` stud round poles lashed with rope wraps at the top) suspending a cast-iron kettle pot (`2x2x2` studs, `#2A2A2E`) directly over the fire pit.
2. **Stone Kerb Hearth Patio**: Inset a dedicated circular stone patio (`diameter 18 studs`, smooth flagstone texture) bordered by a raised rim of 16 rough stone blocks (`1.5x1x1` studs, `#8A8296`) to isolate the fire from the general cobblestone floor.
3. **Flames, Firewood & Cozy Blanket**: Build a base of 6 criss-crossed charred logs (`0.7x0.7x4` studs) with stylized flame parts (`PointLight Range 22`, `Brightness 3`, `#FFAA44`), stacked kindling on the side, and drape a folded rose-pink blanket (`2x1.5x0.3` studs, `#F4A6B8`) over the bench.

#### 8. Whole Hub (Overview, Spawn & Backdrop)
1. **Cathedral Root Canopy**: Double the density and curvature of the World Tree roots so they arch completely overhead and meet at a vaulted ceiling crown, eliminating visible black sky void; thicken trunk roots to `8–12` studs diameter in rich plum (`#3D2645`) with tufted moss clumps (`#589B74`).
2. **Paving Hierarchy & Paths**: Break up the monolithic purple cobblestone noise by laying `4`-stud-wide smooth stone flagstone paths radiating from the spawn well to each shop, leaving gravel or low moss along the borders.
