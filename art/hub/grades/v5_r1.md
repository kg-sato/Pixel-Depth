#### Top 5 Concrete Fixes for Town Layout
1. **Break the geometric radial symmetry**: Shift the mine gantry off-center and cluster structures into asymmetrical neighborhoods (e.g., an intimate market row west, a raised living quarter east) rather than equal-radius spokes.
2. **Carve multi-tier vertical terraces**: Introduce 2–3 distinct ground elevations (a sunken pond/mine basin, a mid-level market, and elevated clifftop perches with retaining walls and winding timber/stone stairs as in [`pdv5_town_s71.jpg`](file:///C:/Users/kgsat/AppData/Local/Temp/agy_sandbox_bfk05za8/Games/Pixel%20Depth/art/hub/pdv5_town_s71.jpg)).
3. **Route the stream through the town center**: Meander the water directly between the central plaza and stalls with stepping stones and varied bridges so paths organically contour around the water.
4. **Eliminate dead grass voids with dense prop clusters**: Clutter the dead ground between the mine shaft and stalls with mining carts on tracks, stacked ore sacks, barrel clusters, tool racks, and canopies.
5. **Vary path widths and blend edges**: Flare path widths at junctions, create a secondary pedestrian loop connecting building-to-building, and replace sharp voxel path boundaries with scattered cobblestone-to-grass dithering.
