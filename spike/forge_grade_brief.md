# Blind grade: one forging-story cutscene from a Roblox game

You get three pictures:
1. A contact sheet of frames captured from the cutscene in the game engine, in story order (left to right, top to bottom). The dark bars with captions are the cutscene's own letterbox.
2. The SETTING reference the scene was built to match (a concept picture).
3. The MAKER reference: a turnaround sheet of the character who makes the pickaxe.

The cutscene tells where a pickaxe came from in three chapters: BORN, MADE, NAMED. It runs in real time in Roblox, so judge it as game graphics for a stylised children's game, against the references.

Be harsh: 5 = average, 7 = good, 8 = rare. Give whole numbers 1 to 10 for each item, then one sentence of the worst fault for that item:

- SETTING: does the built place match the setting reference (landforms, key objects, depth from foreground to far background)?
- CHARACTER: does the maker match the turnaround (shape, colours, clothing) and look appealing?
- LIGHTING: mood, shadows, readable subject, no blown-out or muddy frames.
- COMPOSITION: camera framing of each frame; is the subject clear and uncropped?
- STORY: can you tell from the frames alone what happens in BORN, MADE and NAMED?
- EFFECTS: weather, particles, glow, water; do they add life without hiding the subject?
- OVERALL.

End with the three changes that would raise the score most, most important first. Plain text, no preamble.
