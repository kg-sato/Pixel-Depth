Blind harsh grade of creature pet ANIMATION and VFX in a Roblox voxel game, from contact sheets of slow-motion stills.
Each sheet is a grid of stills in time order; each tile is labelled with the script time in seconds and the state (idle, walk,
run, stop, turn on the spot, act <name>, hero act). The camera orbits slowly. The pets are detailed voxel creatures.

The owner's direction (binding): "the animations move XYZ not just 2 dimensional up and downs. They should feel alive and
replicate real movement of creatures" in every state (idle, walk, run, stop, turn, acts), and the VFX are unique to each pet's
theme, animated and glowing, richer for rarer pets.

Judge from frame-to-frame changes: does the body shift sideways, turn (yaw) and roll, not only bob up and down? Do the legs
plant and lift like a real gait (feet flat on the ground in stance, lifted and carried forward in swing), does the body lean
into turns, does the head move independently (looks around, stays steady while walking), do tails follow through? Is the idle
alive (breathing, weight shifts, head looks)? Do the acts read clearly with anticipation, action and settle? Are the VFX
themed, readable, animated and not cluttered?

Anchors (be harsh: 5 = average, 7 = good, 8 = rare). Score each 1-10: (1) Locomotion realism (walk/run/turn), (2) Idle life,
(3) Acts (clarity, weight, 3D motion), (4) VFX (theme, motion, readability). OVERALL = the lowest score, capped at the mean + 1.
Answer as a markdown table per pet: Pet | Locomotion | Idle | Acts | VFX | **OVERALL** | the 3 most important fixes, each an exact
animation instruction (which body part, which axis, how much, when). Then the mean OVERALL.
